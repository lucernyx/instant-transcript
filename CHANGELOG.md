# Changelog

## 1.0.1 (2026-10-23)

- Fixes the transcript language on videos that YouTube auto-dubs into other languages. Those videos carry an auto-generated track for every dubbed language, and the extension could pick one of them (for example Arabic) instead of the language actually spoken. It now reads the language of the original audio track.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-23)

First release.

- Transcript button next to Like and Share on YouTube watch pages.
- Transcript panel in the right column, under the video in narrow windows and theater mode.
- Highlights the line being spoken and scrolls along; click a line to jump there.
- Copy the whole transcript, with timestamps or as paragraphs.
- Download as a .txt file named after the video.
- Picks the spoken language automatically: human-written captions first, then auto-generated.
- Light and dark themes.
